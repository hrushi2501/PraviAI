# Assumptions and open questions

- Departments are tenants in one governing authority for the hackathon. Central grants cover only that authority.
- Fixed infrastructure includes road sections, bridges, buildings, reservoirs and permanent water facilities. Moveable equipment and fleets are excluded.
- Department experts choose inspection intervals and lifecycle policy references; no national engineering interval or safety score is invented.
- Seven interface languages are a foundation: English, Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil and Telugu. Navigation labels are bundled; domain descriptions and most forms remain English. Professional translation review and full coverage are pending.
- Every displayed asset, person, cost and observation is synthetic. The reference date is 28 September 2026, intentionally fixed for a repeatable recording.
- Mock edits persist only while the app layout stays mounted, and reset after refresh. Approval controls demonstrate independence and do not replace database enforcement.
- Reviewer questions: which owning departments and manuals? Which region hierarchy? Who certifies condition? Which actions require sanction? What is the authoritative inventory source? What constitutes acceptable completion evidence? Which locales have priority? Is the central view operational or planning-only?
- Attachment examples are advisory inputs. Unsupported generic SaaS ideas are adapted to the agreed government scope and actual SQL capabilities.
